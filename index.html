Welcome

A Smile is an inherent human reaction which comes naturally to every person. We feel good, energetic and positive when we see smiles all around us.

The smile of innocence in children, the smile of self pride on the face of a child who has accomplished something special,the beauty and innocence of a baby's smile,the joy and sparkle of the happiness in the smile of a parent or grand parent who holds a new born child.

Well a smile can express many a happy moment.

We at Anuvikaz have pledged to bring a smile on many faces young and old through our various programs.

So welcome to this movement aimed at bringing smiles all around us.
