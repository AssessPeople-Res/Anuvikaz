Age Care Centres

AmarPrem - An Old Age Home inaugurated at Neelangarai on 14th April 2015.

At the old age, when we think that it is the dusk of life, AmarPrem promises a dawn for the aged people who had been running all through their life and now their legs are tired. Amar Prem is the home for the destitute old age persons who will live peacefully their present and we aim to give them the dignified life. The following services are given: To give the inmates a balanced diet. To give them comfort and compassion. To give them good clothing and bedding. To give them proper medical treatment. To give them a simple occupational therapy. To give facilities for recreation. To give them a honourable cremation after death.
