Corporates nowadays embrace Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) as a process by which they can be responsible for the company's actions and encourage a positive impact through its social activities on the environment.

Worldwide corporates have been the major providers for many social causes. The SRM foundation gladly encourages corporates to dedicate their resources for a social cause – be it time, sponsoring events, volunteers, finance to reach the right people at the right time and for the right cause.

Since we are a registered foundation, the donations you make will be eligible for tax deduction under Sec 80G of the Income Tax Act, 1961.
