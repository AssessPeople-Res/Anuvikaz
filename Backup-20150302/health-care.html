Health Care

A nation like India which has over 1.2 billion population spends less than 3 percent of its total expenditure on healthcare. Heedful to the situation prevailing, we at Anuvikaz aspire to do our bit to bring in an environment of health and fitness. The healthcare initiatives of Anuvikaz focus on issues related to health, fitness and general well-being.

Eye care is a core focus area for the foundation. There are more than 12 million blind people in India. Because of extreme sun and genetics, Indians develop cataracts in their 40s and 50s versus 60s and 70s in the United States. Without surgery they go blind, losing many of their productive years. At Anuvikaz, over the years, we have worked with specialists in conducting screening camps in remote villages in Tamil Nadu to detect and treat eye problems and have helped restore vision to hundreds of people by funding cataract surgeries.

Our foundation actively supports Chennai Vision Charitable Trust run by Rajan Eye Care Hospital, one of India's premier Super Speciality eye hospitals in their efforts to eradicate curable blindness in rural Tamil Nadu.
