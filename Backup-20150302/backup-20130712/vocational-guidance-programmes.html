Vocational Guidance Programmes

Why do Painters become Lawyers?

This is the classic question we as parents, teachers and the nation as a whole need to be asking ourselves. It has been clearly established through global research that if one chooses a career based on one’s interest and preference, the chances of his/her success and satisfaction are very high.

There are various reasons as to why vocational guidance has not yet taken roots as a core activity in schools, but its importance is now being seen and recognized by schools, parents and students themselves.

There is a need to make vocational guidance contemporary, relevant, creative and enjoyable. We at Anuvikaz are very passionate about helping students making informed career decisions and have been working in this area for more than a decade now. We help under-privileged students by offering scientific career planning and guidance services and in hand holding them till they make the right career decisions.
