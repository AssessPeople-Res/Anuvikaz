Mother Earth Programmes

Consider this:

  • One billion people in the world have no clean water
  • Two billion people have inadequate sanitation
  • One and a half billion people (mostly in large cities of newly industrialized countries) breath air that is dangerously unhealthy
  • Hundreds of millions of poor farmers struggle to make a living on poor land
  • Whole countries are on the verge of famine

As human beings, we owe our existence to mother earth and it becomes our duty to protect and nurture the environment for future generations. Individuals can contribute to making the world a better, safer, and greener place.

At Anuvikaz we aim to initiate and participate in programmes in the areas of renewable energy, community outreach, land management and so on. The following are some specific focus areas; the foundation is currently working on:

  • Pedal Power
  • Solar Power
  • Bio Digester
  • Composting
  • Organic Gardening
  • Solar Cooking
