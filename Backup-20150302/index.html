About Anuvikaz

Anuvikaz stands for the Sanskrit word - 'to penetrate with one's vision'.

Anuvikaz is an initiative of the Saroja Ramachandran Memorial Foundation (SRM). The SRM Foundation’ was founded in 2004 with the object of being of service to people of this world.

Anuvikaz is an endeavor aimed at making a difference to the lives of people across age groups through various initiatives.

We thank you for visiting Anuvikaz and look forward. to your spreading the word around and getting as much support for our initiatives.
