Welcome

Anuvikaz Sanskrit means 'to penetrate with one's vision'.

Anuvikaz is an initiative of the Saroja Ramachandran Memorial Foundation which was founded in 2004 as a 'Vision for a better world'

Anuvikaz is an endeavor aimed at making a difference to the lives of people across age groups through specific and high impact initiatives. We thank you for visiting Anuvikaz and look forward to your spreading the word around and getting as much support for our initiatives.
