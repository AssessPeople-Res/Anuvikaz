About Founder

RAMACHANDRAN KANNAN

A HR professional by qualification, with 33years of experience, Kannan is an entrepreneur by heart and instinct
(Founder-Promoter of AssessPeople).

An alumnus of XLRI (Batch of 1982), Kannan has had his grounding with organisations like Blow Past, Crompton Greaves, Hindustan Lever and the Murugappa Group.

Kannan founded a not-for-profit trust called Anuvikaz (www.anuvikaz.org).

The objective is to educate and empower underprivileged sections of society by creating reading rooms, offering employment training/vocational guidance and language workshops.
