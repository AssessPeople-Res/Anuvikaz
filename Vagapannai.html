Construction of Sanitation facility for Tribal Village - Vagapanai



Nilgiris District, Tamil Nadu



About Vagapanai Tribal Village


  • Vagapanai Village located in Beautiful Nilgiri Mountains. For the 40 Irula families living at Vagapanai in Kengarai along the Kil Kotagiri slopes, existence is an uphill struggle.
  • People have to walk seven kilometres from their village to reach the nearest bus stop or access basic amenities, including healthcare.
  • If they arrive after dusk, it becomes dangerous to trek to the village because of the presence of the wildlife, including elephants, gaur and sloth bear.
  • Vagapanai village is not just a place; it's an experience—a journey back in time, a celebration of nature, and a warm embrace from the heart of Nilgiri.

About Vagapanai Tribal Village



  • Village name: Vagapanai.
  • District : The Nilgiris
  • State : Tamil Nadu.
  • Language : Tamil and Badaga, Kannada, Hindi.
  • Population profile: Irula Tribals. A population count of approximately 200
  • Elevation / Altitude: 823 meters. Above Seal level.
  • Comes under Kengarai Panchayat, in Nilgiri District.
  • It is a tribal village of Irulas.
  • It is about 7-8 kms from Kootada Tea Estate ,managed by the Stanes Group.
  • The village has about 40 families currently residing.
  • The current Panchayat President : Mr. Murugan.

Objective of the Project


Providing toilets and bath facilities for around 25 families in the Vagapanai Village, Nilgiris and two toilets at major junctions

Features of the Project


  • Initial research on various toilet models available in market which can be carried easily to the village and assemble.
  • Finalizing the model design and outsourcing the fabrication work.
  • Conducting Gramasabhas to educate tribal community on benefits of toilet usage and proper sanitation.
  • Transportation of fabrication materials to tribal village.
  • Fabrication of toilet and bath facility within one box and installation of electric and plumbing connections.
  • Installation of first model at Honnatty Junction.
  • Digging pits for safe disposal of toilet waste.

Photos of Grama Sabha



Installation of First Model Toilets



  • The initial model has been installed at the Honnatty bus stop, a key junction linking 9-10 villages.
  • It serves as a waiting point for women from neighbouring villages to catch buses to nearby cities..
  • However, the lack of adequate restroom facilities has been a pressing issue..
  • First model toilet was installed at this place..
  • The beneficiaries of this project are all women residing in the surrounding villages.
